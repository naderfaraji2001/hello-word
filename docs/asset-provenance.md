## Integration architecture

The presentation slides 3–5 and 8–9 and enablement-guide before/after topology establish multiple clinical sources converging on middleware, bidirectional orders/results, LIS/instrument interfaces and a separate billing/RCM branch. `IntegrationDiagram` redraws that topology in the site design system; it does not turn the systems into a fabricated serial chain. Source vendor lists are not presented as universally available connectors. Case diagrams remain qualitative and illustrative.
